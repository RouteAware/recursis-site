Pitch Recursis

Got an idea? Run it back with us.

Have an app or website idea you can't build alone? Pitch it. A person reads every submission — and when an idea fits how we build, we'll reach out to build it with you or partner up and get it running for real.

How it works

/ 01

You pitch it

The form below. Plain words beat pitch decks — what it does, who it's for, why it matters.

/ 02

We actually read it

A person, not a filter. We're a small shop, so we're honest about capacity: we reply to what genuinely fits.

/ 03

If it fits, we build together

We'll reach out and shape it with you — a straight build, a partnership, or something in between. Terms get decided together, on paper, before any work starts.

/ 04

What fits here

Small, sharp, honest, useful — the same bar as everything in the portfolio. No hype machines, no dark patterns, nothing that sells user data.

The pitch

The fine print

Read before you pitch — it protects us both. Submissions aren't confidential, so don't send trade secrets. Sending an idea doesn't create a partnership, a payment obligation, or any agreement — those only exist when we've both signed something. We're always building, so we may already be working on something similar to what you send. And you keep your idea: pitching costs you nothing and takes nothing from you.
