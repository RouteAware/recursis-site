Privacy

What this site collects, and how to say no.

Plain words, no tricks. Effective September 6, 2026.

The short version. recursisdigital.com is run by Recursis Holdings. We collect what you type into our two forms, cookieless page-view counts, and — only if you press OK when we ask — a Meta ad pixel. We don't sell your data, we don't trade it with data brokers, and a person answers info@recursisdigital.com.

What we collect and why

Forms. The pitch form asks for your name, email, an idea name, the pitch, and optional links. The hire form asks for your name, email, a project description, an optional budget, and optional links. Each submission is turned into an email and sent to our inbox through Resend, our email provider, with your address set as reply-to so we can answer you. We use it to reply, and for nothing else.

Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views and measure how fast pages load. They are cookieless, don't build a profile of you, and don't follow you across other sites. We see aggregate numbers — pages, countries, device types, load times — not people. How Vercel handles it.

The ad pixel — off until you say yes. We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to tell whether our ads bring people here. It does not load until you press OK on the bar at the bottom of the page. Before that there is no request to Meta, no fbevents.js, no pixel cookie, and nothing about your visit leaves this site. If you say yes, the pixel sets cookies and sends Meta the pages you view here along with your browser and IP details, and we always send Meta the Limited Data Use flag, which tells Meta to process that data under California’s restrictions. We remember your answer in your browser for 12 months and then ask again. Change it any time with Change my choice in the footer, or the switch below — and we honor your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal automatically, in which case we never ask at all. Meta’s privacy policy covers what Meta does with what it receives.

The merch shop. shop.recursisdigital.com is hosted by Fourthwall, which is the merchant of record: Fourthwall takes your payment, prints, ships, and handles returns under its own privacy policy and terms (linked in the shop footer). We can see order details in the shop dashboard so we can help with a problem; we don't get your card number.

Our other ventures. The products in the portfolio (TOCTalk, KitID, Lane Alerts, PokéSpa and the rest) are separate sites and apps with their own privacy policies. This page covers recursisdigital.com only.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data, and we never hand it to data brokers.
  • We don't combine what you type into a form with the ad pixel or analytics.
  • We don't send marketing email. If you write to us, we write back — that's it.
  • This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Say no to the ad pixel — it is off until you press OK, and the switch below turns it back off whenever you like. Global Privacy Control in your browser does the same thing automatically.
  • See, correct or delete anything you've sent us: email info@recursisdigital.com from the address you used and we'll do it, usually within a few days. No account, no forms, no proof-of-identity theatre beyond confirming it's your email.
  • Block cookies in your browser if you'd rather — the site works fine without them.

Your ad-pixel choice

The Meta ad pixel is off on this browser until you turn it on. This is the same switch as the bar at the bottom of the page and the Change my choice link in the footer — press it to turn the pixel on, press it again to turn it off. We keep your answer in this browser for 12 months, then ask again. This also covers California’s “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” right: saying no here is that request.

Changes and contact

If we change what we collect, we change this page and the date at the top. Questions, requests, or something that looks wrong here: info@recursisdigital.com. Recursis Holdings, United States.
