02How we build

/ 01

Honest copy, always

A product page never claims what the product doesn't do. If a feature is coming, we say "coming." If something is rough, we say so.

/ 02

Your data isn't the product

We never sell user data. Where our products earn money, it's from attention and value we create — sponsorships, services, useful features — not from you.

/ 03

Small on purpose

A focused portfolio, each product designed and operated by the people who built it. Small enough to care about every screen.

/ 04

Ship carefully

Every change climbs a ladder — built, tested, staged, then released — with a way back written down before it ships.
