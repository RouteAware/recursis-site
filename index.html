02What we build

From a one-page joke site to a native app that takes real payments. The portfolio above isn't just what we own — it's the range, and each one is a different kind of product built end to end. Simplest rung first.

  1. / 01

    A one-page site that lands one idea

    One screen, one point, nothing to scroll past. Never Watch iCarley is a deadpan public-service parody — the whole product is the page, and it still has a domain, a design, and search traffic to earn.

  2. / 02

    An affiliate funnel with something worth reading around it

    PokéSpa is written articles, free tools people actually use — card values, show finder, drop radar — and affiliate links placed where they help instead of where they interrupt. Content in the front, commerce in the back.

  3. / 03

    A print-on-demand storefront wired to ads

    Art, product pages, storefront, and the Meta ad funnel pointed at them — no inventory, no warehouse. Terrible Shirt Co runs this way, and so do the merch shops behind our other brands.

  4. / 04

    A full-catalog store with hands-off fulfilment

    Gear Wizard Armory is a real Shopify catalog: product data, pricing rules, checkout, and orders that get placed with the supplier automatically instead of by hand. The boring part is the part that has to work.

  5. / 05

    A phone app that ships to both stores

    TOCTalk is a community app for coworkers, live on the App Store and Google Play; KitID ships a native app of its own. Review queues, signing, updates, crash reports — the parts of app work nobody puts on a slide.

  6. / 06

    Software people pay for

    KitID identifies tactical gear from a photo and builds a kit around it, with payments running through Stripe. Lane Alerts sends freight brokers a plain-language risk read on a lane. Accounts, billing, email delivery, uptime — held up by us, not a plugin.

Same hands on every rung. Designed, built, deployed, and still run in-house — and the ones further up the ladder, like RouteAware, LinkCart, and Vivaris, sit in the portfolio above with their real stage on them, because that's the rule here. If one of these rungs is the thing you need, tell us which: info@recursisdigital.com.

03How we build

/ 01

Niche is the strategy

Campus coworkers, freight brokers, card collectors, tactical kits — we go deep into markets the big players skip, and build exactly what those people need. Deep beats broad.

/ 02

Prototype fast, ship real

An idea becomes a working product in days — built from day one on production-grade infrastructure. Every release still climbs a ladder (built, tested, staged, shipped), and what wins scales without a rewrite.

/ 03

Honest copy, always

A product page never claims what the product doesn't do. If a feature is coming, we say "coming." If something is rough, we say so.

/ 04

Your data isn't the product

We never sell your data. We run an ad pixel only if you say yes to it — here’s the switch. Where our products earn money, it's from attention and value we create — sponsorships, services, useful features — not from you.
